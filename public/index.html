Questions, answered.

Is buildbyte really free?

Yes. Building, deploying, and hosting on a buildbyte.buzz subdomain is free forever. Custom domains are free during the launch period.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Describe your site in plain language and the AI handles the rest. A visual editor lets you refine anything afterward.

Where is my site hosted?

On Cloudflare's global edge network — 300+ cities, automatic SSL, sub-50ms TTFB almost everywhere.

Can I export my code?

Every site is standard HTML, CSS, and TypeScript. Export the full source anytime.
